## Steps to reproduce
1. Log in with a valid account.
2. Open Cattle Market.
3. Choose the sell option.
4. Enter a very long text (500+ characters) in a text field.
5. Fill in the other fields and submit.

## Test data
500+ characters

## Expected result
The input should be limited or accepted safely, with no crash or broken layout.

## Actual result
The long input is not accepted, and the app crashes or the layout breaks.
